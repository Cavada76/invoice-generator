# Privacy Policy – invoice-generator

_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

This plugin is a set of instructions and a local script for Claude. It has no server and the author collects no data.

**What it reads:** the details you give it for an invoice (your and your client's names, addresses, emails, tax IDs and bank details) and, if present, `invoice-profile.json` in your folder.

**What it writes:** the invoice PDF, and `invoice-profile.json` with your business and payment details if you agree to save them. Files are written only to your own computer or Claude workspace.

**What it sends:** nothing. The script makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking. If a required Python library is missing, Claude may install it from PyPI; no personal data is sent when that happens.

**Retention:** the author receives and retains no data. Files the plugin creates stay where they were saved until you delete them.

**Claude itself:** your conversation and the files you share with Claude are handled by Anthropic under its own privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/Cavada76/invoice-generator/issues
